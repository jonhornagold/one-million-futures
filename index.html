About the review

The Milburn Review, formally Young People and Work, is an independent review commissioned by the Department for Work and Pensions. It is led by Alan Milburn, a former Labour MP and Secretary of State for Health.

It looks at why around one million 16–24 year olds are NEET (not in education, employment or training), what this costs young people and the country, and what should change.
